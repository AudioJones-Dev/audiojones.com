# AJ Digital Business Memory Offer & Pricing Spec v1.0

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the canonical positioning, scope, pricing hypothesis, qualification rules, delivery boundaries, and public-site messaging for the **AJ Digital Business Memory Bootstrap**.

The service transforms fragmented files, undocumented founder knowledge, conflicting procedures, scattered system data, and implicit decision rules into a structured, governed knowledge system that humans and AI can reliably use.

This is **not** folder cleanup, a generic AI workshop, or automatically a RAG/CRM/automation/custom-software implementation.

**Market-facing category:** AI-Ready Business Knowledge System  
**Canonical AJ Digital offer:** AJ Digital Business Memory Bootstrap  
**Parent category:** Founder Intelligence Systems

## 2. Strategic thesis

AI connectivity is not equivalent to business understanding. Connecting an AI system to CRM, accounting, email, calendar, files, or other software can expose records, but those systems rarely contain the full semantic operating context of a founder-led business.

Business Memory should make explicit:

- what the business does and does not do;
- who it serves;
- how it prices;
- how work moves;
- where authoritative facts live;
- which rules govern decisions;
- who owns decisions;
- what requires approval;
- what is known, unknown, conflicting, inferred, assumed, or stale.

The durable asset is **governed business context**, not the number of folders or agents.

## 3. Core principles

1. **Business ontology is discovered, not imposed.** Do not force every client into a generic six-folder structure.
2. **One authoritative home per material fact.** Derived representations may reference canonical facts but should not create competing truths.
3. **Missing context remains explicit.** Do not silently invent information. Track known, inferred, assumed, unknown, conflicting, and stale/unverified states.
4. **Context precedes consequential automation.** Narrow automations may not require a full Bootstrap, but cross-business AI reasoning requires stronger context readiness.
5. **Agents consume governed context.** Agents do not own canonical business truth.

## 4. Target customer

Primary initial ICP: founder-led service businesses with approximately **10–75 employees**, recurring workflows, multiple source systems, concentrated founder knowledge, and active plans for AI, automation, delegation, onboarding, succession, or scale.

Strong signals include founder dependency, repeated inconsistent answers, multiple versions of pricing/policy/processes, slow onboarding, failed delegation, tribal knowledge, disconnected systems, planned AI deployment, turnover risk, and leadership willingness to resolve contradictions.

Disqualify or tightly constrain buyers seeking cheap folder cleanup, refusing stakeholder participation, lacking an accountable sponsor, refusing ownership decisions, or expecting broad custom software/security/migration inside a base Bootstrap.

## 5. Commercial model

Default model: **Diagnostic → Fixed-Price Build → Optional Managed Intelligence**.

A paid diagnostic is preferred when scope, document quality, workflow count, stakeholder count, or contradiction density is unknown.

**Permitted range:** $2,500–$5,000

Typical inclusions:

- up to two additional stakeholder interviews;

- founder-dependency findings;
- source-of-truth gaps;
- contradiction register;
- open-question register;
- AI-readiness risks;
- proposed ontology;

- fixed-price implementation proposal.

AJ Digital may credit **50%–100%** of the diagnostic fee toward implementation within a defined 30–60 day period. Credit policy must be explicit in the proposal.

## 11. Margin and delivery rule

AJ Digital SHALL NOT lock final pricing solely from competitor research.

The first paid deployments must track actual labor by workstream: discovery, interviews, evidence review, extraction, structuring, ontology/taxonomy, AI-assisted production, human QA, reconciliation, revisions, training, and project management.

### Problem framing

**Connected doesn't mean understood.**

AI integrations can expose records from your CRM, inbox, accounting platform, calendar, documents, and other software. They do not automatically explain which information is authoritative, which policy is current, who can approve what, how your team actually works, or where business rules conflict.

Before consequential AI can reason across your company, the company itself has to become legible.

### Public method

1. **Discover** — inspect how the business actually operates.
2. **Map** — identify systems, workflows, people, decisions, and knowledge.
3. **Reconcile** — surface contradictions, unknowns, stale facts, and duplicated truth.
4. **Structure** — create an AI-readable and human-usable knowledge architecture.
5. **Govern** — assign sources of truth, owners, permissions, and approval boundaries.
6. **Activate** — prepare the context for assistants, automations, and Founder Intelligence systems.

## 14. Funnel role

Business Memory is not required to sit behind one universal funnel.

- Revenue/operational constraint → ReKonr or diagnostic-led intervention.
- AI readiness/knowledge fragmentation → Business Memory Diagnostic → Business Memory Bootstrap.
- ReKonr may prescribe Business Memory where knowledge fragmentation is itself a binding operational constraint.
- Appropriate downstream systems may include ResponseOS, Worksie, or other bounded applications.
- Ongoing intelligence may move into Managed Founder Intelligence.
