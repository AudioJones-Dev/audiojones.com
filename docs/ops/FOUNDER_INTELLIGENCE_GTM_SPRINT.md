## Daily lead operating procedure

1. Review the Founder Intelligence lead notification inbox every business day.
2. Confirm that each notification has a durable Neon lead ID. If notification delivery is unavailable, reconcile directly against the Neon intake table before declaring the queue empty.
3. Triage fit using founder/decision-maker status, service-business model, operating constraint, timeline, and ability to act on a diagnostic.
4. Reply within two business days. Use the lead’s preferred contact method and do not imply acceptance before review.
5. Track: lead ID, owner, received date, response deadline, qualification status, next action, and last-contact date.
6. If HubSpot is used manually, store the website lead ID on the HubSpot record. HubSpot is an operator view for this sprint, not the canonical intake store.
7. Reconcile open leads at the end of each business day. A lead is not closed until its disposition and reason are recorded.

### Qualification states

- `qualified`: founder/decision-maker, material operating constraint, appropriate service-business context, and a credible near-term decision.
- `nurture`: relevant problem but timing, authority, or readiness is incomplete.
- `not_fit`: outside the service-business buyer, seeking generic tool setup, or unable to support a diagnostic engagement.
- `review_required`: conflicting or insufficient information; Audio decides.

## Controlled demo script — 10 to 12 minutes

1. **Problem, 90 seconds:** show how scattered demand and weak follow-up create invisible loss.
2. **Disclosure, 30 seconds:** state that the walkthrough is a fictional persisted sandbox with no live providers or customer result.
3. **Call evidence, 2 minutes:** show the simulated call, redacted transcript, and tenant identity.
4. **Structured facts, 2 minutes:** show service need, qualification facts, urgency, and the illustrative value. State that these are seeded fixtures, not runtime AI extraction.
5. **Decision and action, 2 minutes:** show the seeded workflow and manual appointment. State that no calendar or CRM mutation occurred.
6. **Provenance, 90 seconds:** show audit evidence and the fixed `verified: false` outcome boundary.
7. **Fit discussion, 2 minutes:** ask where the prospect currently loses context, ownership, or follow-up.
8. **CTA, 60 seconds:** invite the prospect to start the Founder Intelligence Diagnostic so the real constraint is mapped before any system is prescribed.

If the database is unavailable, use the visibly labeled static fallback and say that persisted proof is temporarily unavailable. Do not conceal fallback mode.

## Discovery questions

- Where does new demand enter today, and who owns the first response?
- Which leads are hardest to recover after the first missed handoff?
- Can you connect a won job back to its source and follow-up path?
- Which decisions still depend on the founder remembering what happened?
- What would need to be true for a diagnostic to be useful in the next 30–90 days?

## Objection boundaries

- **“Is this live?”** No. The current public proof is a persisted fictional sandbox; live-provider work remains gated.
- **“Does it replace our CRM?”** No. ResponseOS is not positioned as a general CRM.
- **“Is the displayed revenue recovered?”** No. It is an illustrative estimate and verified revenue remains zero.
- **“Can you install it immediately?”** The diagnostic determines scope and fit before any implementation commitment.
- **“Is it autonomous?”** No autonomous production execution is represented by this demo.
