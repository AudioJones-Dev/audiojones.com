## Status

M2 - Constitutional Implementation  
Reference Implementation 001  
Founder ratified

This document defines the cognitive transformation each homepage section must create. It does not write homepage copy and does not authorize production implementation.

## Founder Ratification

Reference Implementation:
RI-001 Homepage Message Architecture

Status:
Founder Ratified

Role:
Reference Implementation

Purpose:
Govern the cognitive, emotional, and decision progression of the homepage.

Authority:
Message Architecture

Constraint:
Every section must create a decision, not merely understanding.

Success:
The visitor leaves each section believing something different than when they entered it.

## Homepage Copy Rules

No section may introduce a new idea. Each section may only advance the visitor to the next decision.

If a sentence does not help create the section's intended decision, it should be removed.

## 1. Purpose

The homepage information architecture defines where information belongs.

The homepage message architecture defines what changes inside the founder's mind as they scroll.

This artifact answers one question:

How does the visitor's mental model change section by section?

## 2. Governing Sequence

The homepage should move the visitor through this belief progression:

```txt
Signal Problem
↓
Founder Intelligence
↓
Diagnosis
↓
ResponseOS
↓
Capabilities
↓
Founder Intelligence Diagnostic
```

If a section does not advance that progression, it should be challenged before copy is written.

## 3. Message Architecture Rule

Every section has one job:

Move the founder from Belief A to Belief B.

Copy should not try to be clever before it proves that transition.

## 4. Section Message Map

### Section 1 - Hero

section:
Hero

purpose:
Create immediate recognition and establish that the visitor is dealing with a signal problem, not merely an AI or growth problem.

visitor_before:
I need AI, automation, more leads, or a better tool.

visitor_after:
I may have a signal problem: I cannot clearly see what is actually happening inside my business.

decision_created:
I should diagnose what is actually happening before buying more technology, automation, or lead generation.

constitutional_reference:
- Reality precedes rhetoric.
- Meaning precedes marketing.
- Judgment precedes automation.

doctrine_reference:
- Founder-led businesses often misdiagnose operational confusion as a tool or growth problem.
- AI should not be introduced before the business understands what reality needs to be observed and acted on.

strategy_reference:
- Preserve the strong homepage anchor: "You don't have a growth problem. You have a signal problem."
- Do not lead with AI automation.
- Primary CTA is Founder Intelligence Diagnostic.

evidence_reference:
- Firecrawl crawl audit found the homepage anchor is strong.
- Firecrawl crawl audit found the site over-indexes on AI language relative to philosophy.

supporting_message:
The first problem is not tool selection. The first problem is visibility into what is actually driving or blocking outcomes.

objection_removed:
"This is probably just another AI consultant."

next_action:
Continue into problem framing or start the Founder Intelligence Diagnostic if already convinced.

### Section 2 - Problem Framing

section:
Problem framing

purpose:
Translate the signal problem into concrete founder symptoms.

visitor_before:
Signal sounds interesting, but I need to know whether it applies to my business.

visitor_after:
Signal problem means missed follow-up, unclear attribution, founder-dependent decisions, scattered knowledge, and busy work that does not compound.

decision_created:
My issue may be operational visibility and follow-through, not simply marketing volume or team effort.

constitutional_reference:
- Meaning precedes marketing.
- Evidence outweighs opinion.
- Systems precede software.

doctrine_reference:
- Operational issues become visible through repeated patterns, not isolated complaints.
- Founder overload, weak handoffs, and unclear attribution are symptoms of a deeper operating problem.

strategy_reference:
- Lead with founder-recognizable business reality.
- Avoid internal doctrine language.
- Avoid long taxonomies.

evidence_reference:
- Website crawl surfaced missed calls, slow follow-up, attribution confusion, and founder bottlenecks as repeated public themes.
- Business Memory pilot notes are expected to capture future visitor objections and sales-call language.

supporting_message:
The symptoms are familiar: leads go cold, decisions live in the founder's head, reports do not explain what to do next, and the team stays busy without knowing what is working.

objection_removed:
"Signal problem is abstract."

next_action:
Prepare the visitor to accept Founder Intelligence as the category that explains and organizes these symptoms.

## 5. Full Mental Model Transition

The homepage should produce this complete shift:

| Start State | End State |
| --- | --- |
| I need AI. | I need clearer perception before AI. |
| I need automation. | I need better judgment and execution. |
| I need more leads. | I may be leaking revenue from the leads I already have. |
| This is probably another AI consultant. | This is Founder Intelligence. |
| I should buy a tool. | I should start with diagnosis. |
| This sounds theoretical. | ResponseOS shows the philosophy can become a working system. |
| They sell one product. | They improve how my business sees, remembers, decides, and acts. |
| Maybe I should learn more later. | I should start the Founder Intelligence Diagnostic. |

## 6. Message Guardrails For Copy

- Every section must have a belief transition.
- Every section must create a decision, not just understanding.
- Do not introduce terminology unless it advances a belief transition.
- Do not make AI the solution before the problem has been reframed.
- Do not make ResponseOS appear before the visitor understands Founder Intelligence.
- Do not make Business Memory a headline category.
- Do not include a section because websites usually have that section.
- Do not let proof claims outrun evidence.
- Do not let internal doctrine language appear without founder-language translation.

## 7. Copywriting Readiness

- The belief transition in each section.
