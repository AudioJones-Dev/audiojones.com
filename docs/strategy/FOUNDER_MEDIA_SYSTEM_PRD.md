---
## 1. Problem
Founder-led service businesses are producing content, podcasts, videos, and social posts without a clear system connecting that media to authority, trust, qualified pipeline, or measurable revenue impact.
The market for podcast and video production remains viable, but standard production-only services are increasingly commoditized. AI tools reduce the value of basic editing, transcription, clipping, and post-production. Local production markets are crowded, and generic studio rental competes mostly on price, location, equipment, and aesthetics.
The opportunity is not to sell “podcast production” or “video production” as standalone services.
The opportunity is to help founders turn their expertise into a measurable media system that supports visibility, authority, discoverability, sales conversations, and business development.
---
## 2. Desired Outcome
Add **Founder Media System** to audiojones.com as a core service system.
The offer should position Audio Jones as a systems-led partner for founder-led service businesses that need media infrastructure tied to business outcomes.
Founder Media System should act as the bridge between:
- Founder Intelligence Systems™
- Signal Audit Framework
- attribution systems
- authority building
- video podcast production
- short-form distribution
- SEO / AEO visibility
- business development intelligence
The offer should not make Audio Jones appear to be pivoting backward into commodity media production.
---
## 3. Strategic Thesis
Most businesses produce content.
Very few operate a media system.
Founder Media System reframes content from an output into infrastructure.
Production is the delivery layer. Authority, trust, discoverability, and pipeline are the business value.

## 7. Offer Architecture
### 7.1 Founder Media Audit
Entry diagnostic.

**Purpose:** Identify visibility gaps, authority gaps, content waste, distribution weaknesses, and attribution blind spots.

**Potential price:** $250–$750  
Can also be used as a promotional/free audit during validation.

**Deliverables:**
- current content audit
- founder positioning audit
- distribution review
- search/AEO visibility review
- media-to-pipeline gap analysis
- recommended media system roadmap

**Primary CTA:** Book a Founder Media Audit

### 7.2 Founder Media Launch
One-time setup/build.

**Purpose:** Build the foundation for a founder-led media system.

**Potential price:** $1,500–$5,000

**Deliverables:**
- founder POV and positioning
- show/content format
- topic pillars
- recording workflow
- publishing workflow
- YouTube/Spotify/LinkedIn setup guidance
- short-form repurposing workflow
- analytics baseline
- attribution setup recommendations

### 7.3 Founder Media System
Core recurring retainer.

**Purpose:** Ongoing strategy, production, distribution, repurposing, and reporting.

**Potential price:** $2,500–$8,000/month

**Deliverables may include:**
- monthly content strategy
- recurring video podcast recording
- short-form clip creation
- LinkedIn post repurposing
- YouTube optimization
- episode/show notes
- website content repurposing
- SEO/AEO topic capture
- campaign/reporting dashboard
- attribution review
- qualified conversation tracking

This is the main business model.
