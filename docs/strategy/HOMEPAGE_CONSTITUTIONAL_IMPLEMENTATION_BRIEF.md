# Homepage Constitutional Implementation Brief

## Status

M2 proof artifact. Planning brief only.

No homepage copy is rewritten by this document. No production files are changed by this document.

## 1. Page Job

The homepage has one job:

Make a first-time founder recognize that their business problem is not primarily an AI, automation, or lead-volume problem. It is a signal, memory, judgment, or execution problem that needs to be diagnosed before anything is built.

The homepage should move the visitor from interest to diagnostic intent.

It should not try to explain the entire AJ Digital ecosystem, sell every offer, or prove every product. It should establish the problem, name the category, show the operating path, and route the visitor to the next diagnostic action.

## 2. Visitor Belief Shift

### Before

The visitor likely believes one of these:

- I need AI.
- I need automation.
- I need more leads.
- I need a better follow-up tool.
- I need someone to fix my marketing.
- I need a smarter system, but I cannot clearly define what is broken.

### After

The visitor should believe:

- My business has a signal, memory, judgment, or execution problem.
- More AI or automation will not fix the wrong operating model.
- Audio Jones has a disciplined way to diagnose what is actually happening.
- Founder Intelligence is the category for this work.
- The right next step is a scorecard or diagnostic, not buying another tool.

## 3. Constitutional Principles Expressed

| Constitutional / Design Principle | Homepage Expression |
| --- | --- |
| Reality precedes rhetoric. | Lead with what founders experience inside the business: missed follow-up, unclear attribution, scattered knowledge, founder bottlenecks, and decision fog. |
| Meaning precedes marketing. | Translate internal concepts into founder language before naming frameworks or products. |
| Research precedes messaging. | Anchor claims in crawl findings, Business Memory, diagnostics, and observed founder pain. Do not use unsupported stats as proof. |
| Judgment precedes automation. | Show that AI is useful only after the business knows what to observe, preserve, prioritize, and act on. |
| Systems precede software. | Frame the work as operating design, diagnostic discipline, and repeatable execution before tool implementation. |
| Technology serves philosophy. | Present AI as a leverage layer inside Founder Intelligence, not as the primary philosophy. |

## 4. Strategic Decisions Implemented

- Founder Intelligence is the category.
- AI is the leverage layer.
- ResponseOS is proof, not the whole philosophy.
- Signal Scorecard / Founder Intelligence Diagnostic are the next-action path.
- The homepage should not lead with "AI automation."
- The homepage should keep the current strong anchor: "You don't have a growth problem. You have a signal problem."
- The homepage should use ResponseOS to make the philosophy concrete, not to shrink the brand into one product.
- The homepage should make the commercial ladder visible without turning the first screen into an offer catalog.

## 5. Commercial Ontology Terms

### Use

- Founder Intelligence
- Signal
- Revenue Leak
- Business Memory
- ResponseOS
- Diagnostic
- Operating Blueprint

### Avoid Leading With

- AI agents
- Automation
- Orchestration
- Multi-agent systems
- AI transformation

### Translation Rules

- Use "what is actually happening inside your business" before "operational reality."
- Use "a system that remembers how your business works" before "Business Memory architecture."
- Use "the information that deserves your attention" before "signal" if the audience needs grounding.
- Use "AI is one tool we use to make that possible" after the category is clear.

## 6. Recommended Homepage Section Order

1. Hero
2. Problem framing
3. Founder Intelligence category explanation
4. Diagnostic path
5. Proof / ResponseOS
6. Business Memory / operating clarity
7. Commercial ladder
8. Trust / about
9. Final CTA
10. Footer narrative

## 7. Section Architecture

### 1. Hero

Purpose:
Establish immediate recognition and category direction.

Doctrine expressed:
Reality precedes rhetoric. AI is not the philosophy. Founder Intelligence is the category.

Visitor question answered:
"Is this person talking about my actual business problem?"

Desired belief:
"This understands what I am dealing with better than generic AI consultants do."

CTA or next action:
Primary: Signal Scorecard or Founder Intelligence Diagnostic. Secondary: See the diagnostic path.

What not to say:
Do not lead with "AI automation," "agents," "transform your business," or a list of tools.

### 2. Problem Framing

Purpose:
Translate the signal problem into concrete founder symptoms.

Doctrine expressed:
Meaning precedes marketing. Judgment precedes automation.

Visitor question answered:
"What does a signal problem look like in my business?"

Desired belief:
"My issue is not just lead volume. It is unclear visibility, inconsistent follow-up, scattered knowledge, and weak decision support."

CTA or next action:
Continue to category explanation.

What not to say:
Do not over-abstract into internal doctrine. Do not overuse "signal" without examples.

### 3. Founder Intelligence Category Explanation

Purpose:
Define the external category in plain founder language.

Doctrine expressed:
Technology serves philosophy. Systems precede software.

Visitor question answered:
"What is Founder Intelligence?"

Desired belief:
"Founder Intelligence is the discipline for seeing what is happening, remembering what matters, making better decisions, and executing consistently."

CTA or next action:
Learn how the diagnostic finds the constraint.

What not to say:
Do not make Founder Intelligence sound like software only. Do not turn it into a vague consulting label.

### 4. Diagnostic Path

Purpose:
Show that the first step is diagnosis, not implementation.

Doctrine expressed:
Research precedes messaging. Judgment precedes automation.

Visitor question answered:
"Where do I start?"

Desired belief:
"The right next move is to identify the real constraint before buying a build."

CTA or next action:
Primary diagnostic CTA.

What not to say:
Do not present multiple competing diagnostics without hierarchy. Do not confuse free scorecard and paid diagnostic.

### 5. Proof / ResponseOS

Purpose:
Make the philosophy tangible through the clearest proof product.

Doctrine expressed:
Products produce measurable outcomes. Implementation proves the Constitution.

Visitor question answered:
"What does this look like in practice?"

Desired belief:
"ResponseOS is an example of Founder Intelligence applied to a revenue leak."

CTA or next action:
Explore ResponseOS or calculate revenue leak, depending on final CTA hierarchy.

What not to say:
Do not make ResponseOS appear to be the whole company or the whole philosophy. Do not frame it as a chatbot.

### 6. Business Memory / Operating Clarity

Purpose:
Introduce the memory and knowledge-preservation layer without becoming academic.

Doctrine expressed:
Knowledge exists to improve judgment. Business Memory preserves what the organization knows.

Visitor question answered:
"Why does my business keep relearning the same lessons?"

Desired belief:
"The business needs a way to remember how it works, not just a way to automate tasks."

CTA or next action:
Continue to the commercial ladder or diagnostic.

What not to say:
Do not over-explain Business Memory as a knowledge graph, wiki, or internal architecture.

### 7. Commercial Ladder

Purpose:
Clarify how a visitor moves from interest to implementation.

Doctrine expressed:
Systems precede software. Implementation follows diagnosis and blueprint.

Visitor question answered:
"What happens after I raise my hand?"

Desired belief:
"There is a disciplined path: scorecard, diagnostic, operating blueprint, system, ongoing intelligence."

CTA or next action:
Start with the first diagnostic action.

What not to say:
Do not turn the homepage into a pricing table or full offer catalog.

### 8. Trust / About

Purpose:
Clarify Audio Jones as translator and operator without centering biography over buyer relevance.

Doctrine expressed:
Audio Jones produces understanding. AJ Digital produces validated knowledge.

Visitor question answered:
"Why should I trust this person?"

Desired belief:
"Audio Jones can explain the problem and AJ Digital can operationalize the solution."

CTA or next action:
Read about the approach or start diagnostic.

What not to say:
Do not lean on legacy creative, podcast, or generic agency positioning.

### 9. Final CTA

Purpose:
Convert recognition into a next action.

Doctrine expressed:
Meaning precedes marketing. Diagnosis before implementation.

Visitor question answered:
"What should I do now?"

Desired belief:
"I should take the scorecard or diagnostic because implementation before diagnosis is risky."

CTA or next action:
One primary CTA. One secondary CTA at most.

What not to say:
Do not create three equal CTAs. Do not use "Book a Call" if the next step is actually application or diagnostic.

### 10. Footer Narrative

Purpose:
Reinforce the category and remove legacy residue.

Doctrine expressed:
Coherence over drift.

Visitor question answered:
"What does this brand stand for?"

Desired belief:
"Audio Jones helps founder-led businesses understand what is actually happening and build systems that act on it."

CTA or next action:
Diagnostic, insights, legal/nav continuation.

What not to say:
Do not include stale studio, generic creative agency, or broad AI consulting language.

## 8. CTA Hierarchy Recommendation

### Recommended Primary CTA

Founder Intelligence Diagnostic, if the user is high-intent and the diagnostic path is ready.

### Alternative Primary CTA

Signal Scorecard, if the homepage is intended to maximize low-friction first conversion.

### Recommended CTA Structure

- Primary CTA: Start the Founder Intelligence Diagnostic or Take the Signal Scorecard.
- Secondary CTA: See how the diagnostic works.
- Product CTA: Explore ResponseOS should appear lower on the page, inside the proof/product section.
- Avoid making Book a Call the top primary CTA unless the page actually routes to scheduling.

### CTA Rule

The homepage should not present scorecard, diagnostic, calculator, apply, ResponseOS, and book-a-call as equal next steps.

## 9. Evidence To Use

- Firecrawl audit found the homepage anchor is strong: "You don't have a growth problem. You have a signal problem."
- The site over-indexes on AI language relative to philosophy.
- `/solutions` has the strongest commercial ladder.
- ResponseOS is the clearest proof product.
- Legal and stale legacy language should not influence homepage positioning.
- The current website has fragmented diagnostic and CTA hierarchy.
- Older repo strategy docs contain useful evidence but conflict with the newly ratified architecture; use them as historical evidence, not automatic current doctrine.

## 10. Risks

- Too abstract for SMB founders.
- Too much internal doctrine leaking into public copy.
- Weak CTA hierarchy.
- Overusing "signal."
- Making Founder Intelligence sound like software only.
- Making AI disappear too much.
- Making ResponseOS look like the entire company.
- Reintroducing generic AI agency language.
- Reintroducing legacy creative, podcast, or studio positioning.
- Turning the homepage into an offer catalog.
