## Commercial-validation threshold

A numerical price may move from `Testing` to `Validated` only when all of the following are documented:

- At least 3–5 comparable qualified clients accept approximately the same price.
- Acceptance does not require exceptional discounts.
- Delivery scopes are substantially similar.
- Implementation and support hours are measured.
- Gross margin meets an Audio-approved threshold.
- Outcomes are credible and the value proposition is understood.
- The price survives at least one formal review cycle.

No gross-margin threshold is filled in here because none was approved in the authoritative 2026-07-31 website execution contract.

## ResponseOS SaaS-readiness threshold

ResponseOS remains a diagnostic-led managed implementation. It cannot be described as SaaS-ready until approximately 8–10 substantially similar implementations are complete and evidence shows standardized onboarding, bounded integration work, bounded support, materially lower founder involvement, understood usage economics, and retention.

## Update protocol

After each qualified engagement, record the accepted price, discount, delivered scope, implementation hours, support hours, provider usage, gross margin, outcome evidence, and renewal state. Do not replace `Unknown` with an estimate unless the value is explicitly labeled as modeled and owned by Audio.
