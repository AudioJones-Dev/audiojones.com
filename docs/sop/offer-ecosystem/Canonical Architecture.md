# Canonical Architecture

> [!info] Use this as the official model. Every offer, page, and funnel maps into it.

```txt
1. Free Lead Gen
   - Free scorecards
   - Free audits
   - Free assessments
2. Paid Diagnostics
   - Revenue Leak Diagnostic
   - AI Readiness Diagnostic
   - Founder Intelligence Diagnostic
   - Content Engine Diagnostic
   - Business Memory Diagnostic
3. Conversion Paths
   A. Workshops
   B. Agent OS Solutions
4. Agent OS Suite
   - ResponseOS
   - ReKonr OS
   - PodcastOS
   - Founder Intelligence System
5. Supplementary Ecosystem
   - Eightee20 Society
   - Signal Room
   - Operator Lab
   - Founder Systems Circle
   - Fourthwall Merch
```

## Why this order
- **Demand before prescription.** Free lead gen creates demand; you never sell a tool before the diagnostic says which system is needed.
- **Prescription before install.** Paid diagnostics produce a decision-ready report, not a sales call.
- **Education is optional, not the path.** Workshops qualify and warm; they can sit before *or* after a paid diagnostic.
- **Agent OS is the destination.** Everything routes toward an installed system, then a retainer.
- **Community + merch orbit the path.** They retain and signal identity — see [[Tier 5 - Supplementary Ecosystem]].

## Strategic truths
- Free diagnostics **generate demand**.
- Paid diagnostics **create prescription**.
- Workshops **educate and qualify**.
- Agent OS installs **solve**.
- Community **retains**.
- Merch **signals identity**.
