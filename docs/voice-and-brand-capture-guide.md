# Voice & Brand Capture Guide

A friendly, read-along script for clients to record their voice. The
recording does two jobs at once:

1. **Trains a voice clone** — clean, varied, natural speech.
2. **Captures brand foundations** — the raw words we turn into a
   `brand.md` afterward.

Total target time: **35–45 minutes**. Take breaks between sections.

---

## Before You Start

A few quick things to set you up for success.

- **Find a quiet room.** No fans, no AC hum, no traffic. A closet with
  clothes works great.
- **Use a decent mic.** Even an iPhone in a quiet room is fine. Keep it
  about a hand's width from your mouth.
- **Sip water first.** Have a glass nearby. Avoid dairy and coffee right
  before recording.
- **Talk like a human.** This isn't a performance. Read like you're
  explaining something to a smart friend.
- **It's okay to stumble.** Just pause, take a breath, and start the
  sentence over. We'll edit.
- **Don't rush.** Slower than feels natural is usually right.

When you're ready, hit record and just work through the sections in
order.

---

## Part 1 — Natural Voice Warm-Up

> Target time: **3–4 minutes**

Loosen up. Talk like you're catching up with a friend.

### 1. Personal Introduction

> Who are you, what do you do, and what do you want people to understand
> about you within the first 60 seconds of hearing you speak?

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

### 2. Everyday Speaking Sample

> Walk through what a typical workday looks like for you — from the
> moment you start your day to the point where you feel like the day
> was productive.

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

---

## Part 2 — Brand Foundation

> Target time: **6–7 minutes**

This is the heart of your brand story.

### 3. Origin Story

> What's the story behind your brand, business, or professional
> identity? What made you start doing this work?

*Speak naturally for about 2 minutes.*

### 4. Why This Work Matters

> Why does this work matter to you beyond making money? What problem do
> you feel personally connected to solving?

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

### 5. Audience

> Who do you serve best? Describe the type of person, client, customer,
> or community that gets the most value from what you do.

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

### 6. Wrong-Fit Audience

> Who is *not* the right fit for your brand, product, or service? What
> kind of client, customer, or situation do you prefer to avoid?

*Speak naturally for about 1 minute.*

---

## Part 3 — Brand Positioning

> Target time: **5–6 minutes**

Now get specific about what you offer and why it stands out.

### 7. Core Offer

> Explain your main offer as simply as possible. What do you help
> people do, fix, achieve, avoid, or understand?

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

### 8. Differentiation

> What makes your approach different from competitors, alternatives, or
> the usual way people solve this problem?

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

### 9. Proof

> What evidence, experience, results, stories, or credibility do you
> have that shows you can deliver what you promise?

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

---

## Part 4 — Voice, Values, and Beliefs

> Target time: **6–7 minutes**

This is where your personality really comes through. Be honest, even
opinionated.

### 10. Strong Beliefs

> What are three things you strongly believe about your industry, your
> customers, or the problem you solve?

*Speak naturally for about 2 minutes.*

### 11. Things You Disagree With

> What are some common beliefs, trends, or practices in your industry
> that you disagree with?

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

### 12. Non-Negotiables

> What are your non-negotiables in business, communication, quality,
> customer experience, or leadership?

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

### 13. Brand Personality

> If your brand were a person in the room, how would people describe
> its personality, energy, and communication style?

*Speak naturally for about 1 minute.*

---

## Part 5 — Stories and Emotional Range

> Target time: **5–6 minutes**

Stories give the voice clone emotional variety. Let yourself feel what
you're saying.

### 14. A Challenge You Overcame

> Tell a story about a difficult moment, mistake, setback, or turning
> point that shaped how you think or work today.

*Speak naturally for about 2 minutes.*

### 15. A Client or Customer Transformation

> Describe a time when someone worked with you or experienced your
> product and came out better, clearer, more confident, or more
> successful.

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

### 16. A Moment of Pride

> What's one moment in your work or life that made you feel proud,
> grateful, or certain that you were on the right path?

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

---

## Part 6 — Sales and Messaging Language

> Target time: **4–5 minutes**

Use the words you'd actually say to a real customer.

### 17. Pain Points

> What are the most common problems your audience is dealing with
> before they come to you?

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

### 18. Desired Outcome

> What does success look like after someone works with you, buys from
> you, or follows your guidance?

*Speak naturally for 1–2 minutes.*

### 19. Objections

> What doubts, fears, objections, or hesitations do people usually have
> before saying yes to you?

*Speak naturally for about 1 minute.*

### 20. Call to Action

> If someone is listening right now and they know they need help, what
> should they do next — and why should they act now?

*Speak naturally for about 1 minute.*

---

## Part 7 — Phonetic Coverage Reading Passage

> Target time: **3–4 minutes**

Read this clearly and naturally. Don't rush. This passage covers a wide
range of sounds the voice model needs to learn your speech patterns.

---

Building a strong brand is not just about looking professional. It is
about creating trust before the conversation begins. People need to
understand who you are, what you stand for, what you offer, and why
your work matters.

A clear brand makes decisions easier. It helps customers recognize the
right solution, understand the value, and feel confident taking the
next step. When the message is confusing, people hesitate. When the
message is specific, honest, and useful, people move forward.

Every business has a voice. Some brands sound rushed, generic, and
forgettable. Others sound focused, grounded, and deeply aware of the
customer's real problem. The goal is not to sound like everyone else.
The goal is to sound unmistakably like yourself, but clearer, sharper,
and more consistent.

A great brand can explain complex ideas in simple language. It can
educate without overwhelming. It can sell without sounding desperate.
It can be confident without being arrogant. It can be warm without
being vague.

The strongest brands are built from truth: real experience, real
values, real proof, and real language from the people behind the work.
That is why this recording matters. It captures not only the sound of
the voice, but the thinking, rhythm, personality, and perspective
behind the brand.

---

## Part 8 — Final Free Talk

> Target time: **3–5 minutes**

Last one. Speak from the heart.

### 21. Final Message

> Imagine your ideal audience is listening to this recording right now.
> What do you want them to know, remember, believe, or feel after
> hearing from you?

*Speak naturally for 3–5 minutes.*

---

## When You're Done

1. **Save the recording** as a single file if possible (`.wav` or
   `.m4a`). If you broke it into parts, name them in order:
   `01-warmup.wav`, `02-foundation.wav`, etc.
2. **Send it over** along with any notes you want us to know (things
   you wish you'd said differently, names to spell, etc.).
3. **We'll handle the rest** — transcription, voice clone training,
   and turning your answers into a draft `brand.md`.

Thanks for trusting us with your voice. Talk soon.
