## Strategic rationale (from CLAUDE.md update during GBP setup)

Audio is actively working the Naples / Fort Myers corridor and has
identified an underserved opportunity: **blue-collar service businesses
(HVAC, plumbing, marine services, roofing, restoration, landscaping,
auto services) with $1M-$10M revenue and minimal AI/automation adoption**.

These businesses are not the same buyer as Miami founders. Different
language, different pains, different objection set. The landing page
copy must reflect that.

## Buyer profile for SW Florida

**Not:** Tech-savvy startup founder shopping for an AI consultant
**Yes:** 45-65-year-old service-business owner who built revenue the hard
way, runs payroll on Wednesdays, has a part-time bookkeeper, runs Google
Ads through "a guy", and has heard about AI but doesn't trust most of
what he sees online.

**What they want:**
- Less time chasing leads, more time on jobs
- Predictable lead flow, not feast-or-famine
- Help with phone tag and missed calls (← AI receptionist is the wedge)
- A view of which marketing dollars actually work

**What they fear:**
- Looking foolish in front of their crew
- Getting upsold by "AI experts" who don't understand their business
- Paying for software that sits on a shelf
- Locking into long contracts

### FAQ (SW Florida version — more concrete, less conceptual)

1. **What does an AI receptionist actually do?**
   It answers your phone 24/7 in English or Spanish, asks the caller a
   few qualifying questions, books the estimate or service call, and texts
   you a summary. Works on your existing phone number — no port required.
   Typically catches 90%+ of after-hours and overflow calls.

2. **How much does it cost?**
   The AI receptionist alone is usually $X-$Y per month depending on call
   volume. Most clients see it pay for itself in the first month of
   recovered calls. Full system installs (receptionist + attribution +
   automation) run $X-$Y for 90-day implementation. *(Fill in actual
   pricing.)*

3. **Will it sound like a robot?**
   No. Modern AI voice tech handles natural conversation fluently. Most
   callers don't realize they're not talking to a human until 30+ seconds
   in. The handoff to your team (when needed) is seamless via text.

4. **What if a caller wants to talk to a real person?**
   The AI handles 80-90% of inbound calls completely. For the rest, it
   collects the request and routes to your team via SMS / dashboard — same
   as your current system, just better-organized.

5. **Do I need to switch software?**
   Usually not. We integrate with what you already use — ServiceTitan,
   Housecall Pro, Jobber, Zoho, custom CRMs, even Google Sheets if that's
   your current stack.

6. **What's the catch?**
   It works best for service businesses doing $1M-$10M in revenue with
   meaningful inbound call volume. Below that, the math doesn't always
   pencil out. We'll tell you upfront if it's not a fit.
