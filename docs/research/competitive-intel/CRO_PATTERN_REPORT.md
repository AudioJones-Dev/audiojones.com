## Current State

Audio Jones has a mature conversion ladder:

- Free AI Readiness Score / Diagnostic
- Revenue Leak Diagnostic
- ResponseOS
- Founder Intelligence System Install
- Managed Intelligence Retainer
- Book a Call / Apply paths

The CRO issue is not absence of offers. The issue is choice density and proof packaging. Competitors often use simpler repeated conversion motions: book a call, get proposal, get quote, contact, or free audit.

## Audio Jones Conversion Gaps

### Facts

- The homepage includes multiple CTA paths: diagnostic, book a call, ROI calculator, ResponseOS, insights.
- `/pricing` publicly lists entry diagnostics, workshops, ResponseOS tiers, larger engagements, and FAQs.
- `/services` frames engagements by diagnose, operate, build, and attribution.
- `/case-studies` exists, but the crawl summary shows fewer proof-specific links than competitor proof libraries.

### Inferences

- The pricing ladder is clear for an already-warm visitor, but cold search visitors may need symptom-based routing first.
- The diagnostic is the strongest lead magnet, but it should appear as the default next step on more educational pages.
- Audio Jones should avoid copying competitor "agency package" framing. The stronger CRO path is "symptom -> diagnostic -> system."

## Quick Wins

1. Add a diagnostic CTA block to every insight/article page.
2. Add a "Not sure where to start?" decision block to `/pricing`.
3. Add proof cards on `/case-studies` that separate audited facts, modeled estimates, and qualitative outcomes.
4. Add a consistent related-link module: framework, diagnostic, proof, pricing.
5. Make the free score the default soft conversion on cold informational pages.

## Risks

- Too many CTAs can dilute the diagnostic-first positioning.
- Overstated proof could conflict with the repo's claim-safety posture.
- Local-service CRO patterns should not be imported unless local acquisition is a real strategy.
