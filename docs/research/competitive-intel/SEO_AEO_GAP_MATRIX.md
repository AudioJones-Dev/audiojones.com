## Executive Diagnosis

AudioJones.com has strong category language around Founder Intelligence Systems, revenue leaks, signal, attribution, and diagnostic-first implementation. The main structural gap is not positioning quality. It is topic coverage and retrieval packaging.

Competitors and references cover more search-shaped pages: cost guides, ROI guides, top-agency comparisons, local service pages, FAQ pages, AI-search guides, and proof libraries. Audio Jones has a sharper point of view, but fewer pages that answer the predictable search and AI-assistant questions around that point of view.
